# Design: justask
Fences from ~/.claude/design/DESIGN.md (never list, craft floor). Everything else is this direction.

The package's React pieces ship unstyled, so this direction binds the demo and any styled example, never the pieces' markup.

## Direction: Mounted specimen
The invoicing app sits in a framed window on a quiet neutral page, the way Cal.com shows its booking widget, so a developer judges justask inside an app like theirs; the hood beside it explains every pick in plain rows.

- References: Cal.com's homepage (the real widget framed on a plain ground is the hero); Stripe Elements (the component shown mounted inside a host app); a specimen mounted on a lab slide, from outside software: the object on show sits on its own raised surface, everything around it is quiet.
- Type: Host Grotesk (variable, weight axis) for everything, 800 for the product name, 600 for headings, 400 to 500 for UI; Fragment Mono only for real data: figures, invoice numbers, dates in tables, code. Reason: a plain modern grotesk that does the job for a developer tool's demo and nothing more; the owner picked it on 2026-09-25 (#131) over Schibsted Grotesk and Public Sans, all three shown on the real demo, to replace Mona Sans, which impeccable's overused-font detector flags as a face AI-made interfaces reach for. It is on neither that list nor the spent faces, stays quiet at UI sizes, covers every Spanish diacritic, and its default digits are equal width, so sans figures line up with no tnum feature (it has none). It pairs with Fragment Mono as Mona Sans did. One family keeps the page from looking designed at.
- Color strategy: Restrained. Cool neutrals near hue 250 at very low chroma, one emerald accent at hue 155: oklch(0.6 0.15 155) for lines, bars, dots and focus on light, oklch(0.8 0.17 155) on dark, never for text; filled buttons use a deeper action step, oklch(0.5 0.13 155), dark enough for white labels in both themes. Reason: the owner chose emerald from green and blue options on 2026-09-25, in the family of Supabase and Neon, and kept it on 2026-09-25 (#131) although it sits 10 degrees from docf's 165, since docf is a private tool for doctors that never shares a screen, an audience or a brand with this demo; the accent means only "justask did this or you can act here".
- Ground: follows the system (Auto) with Light and Dark in the header. Light is a pale cool gray page with a white window; dark is a graphite page with a charcoal window. Neither is cream or warm. Reason: the owner asked for the visitor's own default, and the cool ground is what keeps the page off the Anthropic off-white.
- Shape: 12px on the app window, 10px on the hood's panels, 8px on controls, 6px on chips and small buttons; never a pill. Density: comfortable, 36px controls and a 40px request box on desktop, 44px on touch. Depth: one shadow, on the app window only; everything else is flat with hairlines, save the raised segment in a segmented track, whose 1px contact shadow is the control's convention, and the calendar popover, which floats over the page. Small marks (the pick badge, the probability bars) round at 3 to 4px. Reason: the window is the specimen, so it is the one thing lifted.
- Motion signature: the fill line. When an answer fills a control, the control settles in (translateY 4px to 0 with opacity, 150ms, kept from #123), an emerald line draws along its top edge from the left, one control after another in field order, 50ms apart, and the control's box lights in emerald, then the light fades over 800ms and the line over 1400ms. Placements: the Table's filter controls, the Form card's fields, the chosen search result. Under reduced motion the line and the light fade in and out with no drawing and no settle. It starts once the answer is in and never delays the call. The Table's rows take no part in it: the rows an answer brings in fade in over 160ms, opacity only (80ms under reduced motion), and the rows it takes out go at once, so the table never changes height mid motion (#139). Reason: the sentence fans out into the app's own controls, and the eye can follow the order at a few hundred ms per call (#123, #126).
- Unexpected move: the fill line, the one mark of justask on the host app, which leaves no trace once the answer is read. A field the answer held gets no line: the app cannot tell a held field from one the request never named, by design, so the hood alone says why.

## Tokens
In `demo/src/styles.css`, `:root` and its two dark blocks. Surfaces: `--bg` (the page), `--window` (the app, the hood's panels), `--surface`, `--surface-strong`, `--field`, `--segment-on`. Lines: `--border`, `--border-strong`, `--track`. Text: `--text`, `--muted`. Accent: `--primary` (lines, the picked bar, dots, focus; never text), `--fill-wash`, `--ring` (text selection only). Neutral bars: `--bar`, `--bar-none`. Actions: `--action`, `--action-hover`, `--on-action`. State: `--destructive`. Shape and depth: `--radius-window`, `--radius-panel`, `--radius-control`, `--radius-chip`, `--shadow-window`, `--shadow-segment`. Type and motion: `--sans`, `--mono`, `--ease-out`.

## Decisions carried over
- Stack notes (Tailwind, shadcn, an icon set): the demo uses plain CSS on class names and no icons. Reason: it shows the package's unstyled pieces styled the way any host app would style them, and one page needs no component library.
- No icons, but two: the card page's calendar steps between months with two authored SVG chevrons. Reason: a 32px step in a 264px popover has no room for "Previous month", and the arrows are the convention every calendar teaches; the buttons carry the words as their accessible names.
- App shell (icon-rail sidebar, command palette): the demo has a 56px header with the case tabs, the theme toggle and the language toggle, and nothing else. Reason: one showcase page with three cases and one text box each; a palette would compete with the box the demo exists to show.
- Tables on mobile stay tables: the panel's candidates (two columns). Reason: they fit a 375px screen without scrolling, checked at 390, and have no row actions a card list would carry. Both transactions tables, the Table case's five columns and the Search case's four, become card lists under 560px, as the invariant says; inside the framed window the four no longer fit 375px in Spanish (#126).
- Page title: case tabs (Table, Form, Search) replace the page links beside the product name, the selected one raised in the segmented track, and the h1, the case's name, is visually hidden. Reason: the showcase is one page, the selected tab already shows the case, and the app and hood headings below carry the hierarchy.
- Motion only when the instrument responds to the person: a recorded run, on load, once per browser tab (#135), types its sentence into the box (and presses Enter on the Form case, whose box calls only on Enter), and the controls or fields settle in with no one acting; the Table's answer applies itself, as a live one does, and the replay never presses Save. Reason: the showcase must show a real answer in the first viewport with no click (docs/demo-showcase-brief.md), and a replay the eye can follow proves the controls are the app's own; it is interruptible, ends at the person's first keystroke, pick, control or field change, and under reduced motion the sentence appears whole and the controls fade. A reload that opens the end state at once shows it still: nothing settles in or fades, since nothing typed in (#139).
- URL reflects state (open panel): the case and the language live in the URL, the hood's place does not. Reason: the hood is open beside the app on a desktop and behind a switch on a phone, so one flag would mean two things on the two layouts; a shared link opens each device on its default, the hood open on a desktop and the app first on a phone, and the place is kept while the visitor moves between cases.

- Animate only transform and opacity: the signature motion's light animates border-color and background-color, from the accent back to the box's rest. Reason: the owner asked for an accent highlight on each filled field (#123), and under reduced motion that color change is the whole feedback; it stays on the control's own box, never on layout.
- Confirm for a reversible action: the Table case applies each answer to its controls with no Apply button, and Clear filters undoes it; Confirm stays where a record is created (the Form card's Save). Reason: the owner's call on 2026-09-24 (#123): filtering is reversible, the invariant bans confirming reversible actions, and a first visitor never found the Apply button.
- Suggestions ("Try a request") sit in a raised surface panel with a title-size heading, not under a divider, after the case's result: the Table's rows, the Form's Save, the Search's transactions. Reason: they are the way in, and a first visitor looked past them (#123); above the controls they pushed the replay's payoff below the fold on a phone (#126, the owner's call). Their chips keep the text color, with the accent only on hover.
- A button's label follows its fill: dark on a light or white fill, white on a fill dark enough to carry it at 4.5:1, never black on a fill that could hold white. Reason: the owner's rule, 2026-09-25.
- The Table case's pages: ten rows a page, the range ("1 to 10 of 25", in the data face) and Previous and Next at the end of the Applied row over the table, and its rows 30px tall rather than 36. The page lives in no URL and goes back to the first on any change to the filters, which live in none either. Reason: the owner's call on 2026-09-25 (#134): at 36px with the steps under the table, "Try a request" fell to 866px on an 800px desktop; this way it ends at 793px.
- A held search offers, never picks (#134): "Which one?" lists the vendors a request could mean as buttons as wide as the item, and "No vendor matches" is followed, smaller and chip-sized, by the closest vendors above zero, never ones at zero in catalog order. Neither settles in or draws the fill line, since the person picks and justask filled nothing; a picked one takes an accent border, as a pressed control does. Reason: an empty result punished a slip, and a list ranked by nothing reads as an answer (the owner's call, 2026-09-25).
- The Form's box calls only on Enter, so a Fill button sits beside it and sends the same (#147): it rests quiet, bordered on the field color, and reads as the next step in the action fill, with a hint under the box ("Press Enter to fill the card", tied to it by aria-describedby, no live region), only while the sentence in the box has not been sent. Off, as Save is, while the box is blank. A press by Enter or by the replay shows on it for 150ms. The ↵ keycap teaches the shortcut and hides on touch, where the box's `enterKeyHint` names the keyboard's key. Under 560px it drops below the box, which beside it kept a third of the row. Reason: the owner typed in the deployed Form and did not know it calls on Enter; calling as you type would wipe hand-set fields.
- No accent-colored text: chips, links, status words and labels stay in the text or muted color; the accent rides on a border, a dot, an underline or a fill. Reason: the owner disliked green text across the page, 2026-09-25.

## Rejected directions
- Precision Instrument (cobalt 255, IBM Plex Sans with JetBrains Mono, achromatic, sharp): the fallback the demo shipped with until #126; docmatch landed on the same, so it read as a sibling.
- Answer sheet (violet drop-out ink, bubbles, Archivo): liked, but too costumed for a page a developer operates.
- Punched card and Destination blind: costumes; neither read as a professional developer tool.
- Night shift (warm dark page, mustard): close to the dark-with-one-accent default.
- Berry accent (hue 350): too close to AI purple for the owner.
- Chartreuse (125) and Azure (225): shown with emerald; the owner picked emerald.
- Mona Sans: the demo's face until #131; on impeccable's overused-font list. Schibsted Grotesk and Public Sans: shown with Host Grotesk in #131; the owner picked Host Grotesk.

## Benchmarks
- Cal.com (https://cal.com): the real product widget, framed on a quiet ground, is the hero.
- Stripe Elements (https://stripe.com/payments/elements): the component shown mounted inside a host app.
- Supabase (https://supabase.com): the brand color only on actions and live state.
- Neon (https://neon.com) and Railway (https://railway.com): named by the owner as loved; their one product-specific move is what keeps a dark page with one accent from being generic.
